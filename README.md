# Do Lifecycle-Specific Explanations Change the Decision?

**Calibrating SHAP and LIME driver rankings against refit noise and customer mix in churn models**

Author: Hanaa Parvez Khan

An exploratory study of whether the drivers of churn really differ across customer lifecycle stages, or whether the differences come from the explanation method, the algorithm, refitting, or who is in each stage. Every difference is compared against a calibrated reference: a noise ceiling and a 200-replicate refit distribution.

> **Proxy data, exploratory.** The analysis uses the public IBM Telco customer churn sample as a proxy for subscription behaviour. Nothing here establishes B2B SaaS behaviour. Stages are tenure bands of different customers, not trajectories.

## Headline results

- SHAP and LIME agree closely within a model (Spearman rho 0.83 to 0.99). Random forest vs. logistic regression agree less (rho 0.68 to 0.81), against a noise ceiling of 0.998 to 0.999.
- Composition accounts for 51% to 65% of the fall in churn from Onboarding to Maturity (Kitagawa decomposition).
- Flagged-customer overlap between a pooled and a stage-specific model is 76.5% to 82.9%, inside refit noise.
- Pooled across stages, per-customer top-driver agreement differs from refit noise by +27.8 points (95% interval +7.7 to +46.6); the top-three check gives +16.9 (+4.6 to +29.9). These pooled summaries were added after the stage-level results were seen and are secondary.

Full results, limitations and appendices A to G are in the paper.

## Reproducing

1. `pip install -r requirements.txt`
2. Download the IBM Telco dataset and save it as `data/Telco_customer_churn.xlsx` (see `data/README.md`).
3. Run `notebooks/Telco_Churn_Lifecycle_XAI_Pipeline.ipynb` top to bottom. The random seed is fixed.

The notebook writes its own plots to the folder you run it from.

## Limitations

- Proxy dataset, one snapshot, one train/test split.
- The scripts for the pooled mean gap and top-three check were written separately and are not yet in the notebook.
- The CSV tables and PNG figures in `results/` are taken from the paper, not regenerated by the notebook.
- All results are exploratory.
